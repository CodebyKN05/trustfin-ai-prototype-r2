\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{ABSTRACT}

Loan assessment plays an important role in determining an applicant's eligibility for financial assistance. However, understanding the reasons behind an approval or rejection can be difficult. TrustFin AI is an explainable loan assessment system designed to make this process more transparent for applicants and bank officers.

The system supports home-loan and personal-loan applications by analysing financial details such as income, credit score, existing loan obligations, requested loan amount and repayment period. It uses machine learning to estimate loan approval outcomes and explain the factors influencing each prediction. It also provides financial indicators, including estimated monthly instalments and repayment burden, along with what-if simulations to explore the effect of changes in applicant details.

TrustFin AI offers applicants a simplified assessment summary and provides bank officers with facilities to review applications, compare records, revisit previous assessments and generate reports. Explanation validation checks further examine the reliability and consistency of the generated explanations.

The project demonstrates the use of explainable machine learning in financial decision support. Its development and evaluation are based on synthetically generated loan records, and further testing with real lending data is necessary to establish its practical applicability.


\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Loan assessment, explainable machine learning, loan approval prediction, financial decision support, explanation validation, what-if simulations.
